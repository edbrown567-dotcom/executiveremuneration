The book

Executive Remuneration: Law and Practice, by Edward Brown KC, is forthcoming from Oxford University Press. It states the law in full, with the authorities. This site is its companion: posts are shorter and are written as developments occur.

Order from Oxford University Press

The author

He practises in commercial and employment law, much of it where the two meet. His commercial work includes shareholder and partnership disputes and claims arising from share and incentive arrangements. His employment work includes disputes over bonuses and share entitlements, restrictive covenants and team moves, and the departure of senior executives. Cases of this kind usually require the articles, the plan rules and the service agreement to be read together, and in the financial sector the remuneration rules as well. He also acts in arbitration and in financial services regulatory matters, and is on the list of arbitrators of the London Court of International Arbitration (LCIA). He is a contributor to Company Directors: Duties, Liabilities and Remedies (Oxford University Press).

What the site covers

Posts consider new decisions, legislation, regulatory rules and consultations as they affect the structure, award, adjustment and recovery of executive pay, and the disputes that follow, including those that arise when an executive leaves. They address the law of England and Wales, together with the regulatory rules that apply across the United Kingdom.

Each post states the law as at its date of publication and is not updated afterwards unless it says so. Posts are general commentary, not legal advice, and should not be relied on in relation to any particular matter. The views expressed are the author’s own.
