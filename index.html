Shorter deferral, unchanged clawback

The 2025 reforms cut minimum deferral for material risk takers in dual-regulated firms to four years. The seven-year clawback period did not move, so more of it now falls after pay has vested.

BlueCrest and the limits of significant influence

The Supreme Court has held that an LLP member’s influence counts for the salaried member rules only if it is rooted in the members’ legal rights. Commercial importance is not enough.

Non-financial misconduct and pay

Since 1 September 2026 the conduct rules reach serious bullying, harassment and violence in firms other than banks. The rules say nothing about pay, but they widen the conduct capable of engaging malus and clawback.
